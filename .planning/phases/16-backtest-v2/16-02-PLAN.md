### 2. `gaps.py` — segmentation + haircut

### 3. `grading.py` — evidence grading + look-ahead canary
Grade dataset→promotion-eligibility: only `recorded + complete + long + gap-light` passes; `traded_only`/`fixture`/`hand-authored` → never promotion evidence. Canary: extreme prices after T never change a decision at/before T (point-in-time purity). Tests: `test_grading.py`, `test_lookahead.py`.
