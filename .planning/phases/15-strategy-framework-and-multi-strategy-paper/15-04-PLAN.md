- momentum_breakout: over the last lookback_seconds, if price >= window_high * (1 + breakout_pct/100) then BUY; exit at stop_loss_pct below entry or after max_hold_seconds.
- new_launch_sniper: within max_age_seconds of mint creation, with unique buyers >= min_unique_buyers and curve progress <= max_curve_progress, BUY; exit at take_profit_pct above entry.
- mean_reversion: over window_seconds compute mean and standard deviation of the price tape; if price <= mean - z_threshold * stddev then BUY; exit at the mean or stop_loss_pct below entry or after max_hold_seconds.
