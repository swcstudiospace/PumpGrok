**Action:**
Create `src/pumpgrok/backtest/harness.py` exposing exactly the contract
name `run_search`, the only search entry point: it tunes over
walk-forward folds on the pre-holdout region only, records every trial in

searches flag ineligible and above-50-trial searches raise the typed error
