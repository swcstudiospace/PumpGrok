## Objective

Create the shared result model and extend the replay result payload with
per-fill price, venue fee, network cost, latency, filled fraction and
outcome, so backtest results and paper ledger trades build from one model
and compare over the same window. Per CONTEXT D-01, D-02, D-10 and G-10.
