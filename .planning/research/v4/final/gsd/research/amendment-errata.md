# Errata for the constitution amendment draft (N6 section 5 point 9 and item 1 and 2)

For owner review (OD-01). The draft at `/tmp/v4/design/C-AMENDMENT-DRAFT.md` is NOT edited; these are proposed replacements for the owner to accept, reject or reword. Nothing here is applied by any agent.

**E1 (clause 2, last sentences; N6 items 1 and 2).** In the replacement for the final sentence of clause 2, replace `Under an unexpired, verified arm record the desk may send autonomously for the listed strategies only ... A halt, kill switch, open breaker, strategy kill, demotion, or UNKNOWN hold suspends autonomous sends until a human clears it.` by:

    A live send requires either that exact approval message, or an arm record: a standing authorization the human signs once with the operator key, bound to the exact configuration hash, the wallet allowlist, a list of (strategy, parameter-set hash) pairs, a notional cap of at most 1.0 SOL, and an expiry no later than the configured maximum. Under an unexpired, verified arm record the desk may send autonomously for the listed pairs only, inside the written risk limits and the record's caps, and each send is recorded as an ApprovalRecorded row of kind autonomous_live. Any change of a parameter-set hash requires a new arm record. Agents never create, extend, or widen an arm record. A halt, kill switch, open breaker, strategy kill, demotion, UNKNOWN hold, or arm expiry suspends autonomous ENTRIES until a human clears it or re-arms. Exits for positions already open (stop-loss, take-profit, trailing stop, maximum hold, rug alert, kill flatten) are never suspended by those conditions; they remain inside the wallet allowlist and the notional cap, and each is recorded as an ApprovalRecorded row of kind autonomous_live.

**E2 (clause 3; N6 section 5 point 9).** Add to the clause 3 replacement: `A send that fails on-chain (an execution error) stops all new entries and waits for a human to clear, exactly like an UNKNOWN result; only a result the chain proves never landed (blockhash expired and finalized check) is recorded as rejected without a hold.` (The exec_errors breaker alone auto-resets after 900 s, which would let failed sends resume without a human.)

**E3 (closing paragraph and runbook; N6 section 5 point 9).** Keep `Live capital exists only through the arm procedure of clause 2; no default, overlay, or agent enables it.` and state in the arm runbook that the operator uses a local configuration file outside git named by `PUMPGROK_CONFIG` (never a committed overlay with `enabled=true`).

**E4 (clause 1 and runbook; N6 section 5 point 9, item 28).** The throwaway limit stays 200 USD while the notional cap is 1.0 SOL. Add to the runbook, not to the clause: the arm sets `cap_sol = min(1.0, 200 USD / SOL price minus fees)` at arm time and funding never exceeds that cap plus fees.

**E5 (scope list; N6 section 4 and 5).** Add to the `Scope` list the copies the draft missed: `prompts/bot-04-sniper.xml` (lines 6 and 27), `prompts/bot-00-chief.xml` line 65 and `agents/chief.md` line 72 (`never allow SNIPER without the exact human approval phrase`), `AGENTS.md` line 91, `ARCHITECTURE.md` line 170, `SETUP.md` lines 120, 203, 241, `docs/ROSTER.md` line 116 and the key-custody rows at lines 196, 238, 280, `docs/runbooks/setup.md` line 27, `skills/key-custody/SKILL.md` line 24 (`There is nothing to custody`), and `docs/RISK_POLICY.md` section 3.3 line 128 (`R17: no live auto-exit exists`). Also name the INV-S-01 text change: the documented scope lists `services/` and `scripts/` as exempt; the signer needs an explicit statement.

Why these matter: E1 closes a blocker (as drafted, exits would be suspended exactly when stop-loss and kill-flatten must run); E2 removes a weakening; E3 to E5 remove contradictions between the amendment and the runbook, and incomplete copies that the oracle and `assemble-prompts --check` would otherwise leave inconsistent.
