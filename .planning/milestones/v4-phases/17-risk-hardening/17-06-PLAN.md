## Test strategy

Kill-the-process tests with a temp ledger file, a crash-mid-recovery
test, a corrupt-ledger refusal test, and the resume-buy test. All on a
fake Clock; no network. The no-SKIPPED grep is part of the criterion
because a skipped crash test would hide the exact regression this plan
exists to prove absent.

## Rollback

Remove the recovery pass from the root startup; the desk starts with
empty in-memory state as before. Restored rows remain in the ledger, so
re-adding the pass is a pure revert.
