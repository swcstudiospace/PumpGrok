<objective>
Route every strategy Intent to exec.place through one router that allocates a ticket, obtains an approval from the risk engine, respects the autonomy level, and journals every refusal.

Purpose: owner decision 3. Below autonomous-paper nothing may be approved; the router refuses and records the refusal. The router is the only path from a strategy to a paper order.
Seat: bot-00-chief (supervisor/**). This plan uses ledger, risk and tools through their public functions and edits none of them.
</objective>
