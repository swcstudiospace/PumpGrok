## Test strategy

Fault-injection first: a silent source, a never-ticked source, a
disconnect storm, an UNKNOWN send, and a transient error burst, each on a
fake Clock with zero wall waiting. The idle case asserts no verdict change
after 24 virtual hours with no events. No network; all sources are the
Phase 12 FaultyEventSource fakes.

## Rollback

17 breaker behaviour. The halt rows already in the ledger remain readable
by the old engine because no row shape changed.
